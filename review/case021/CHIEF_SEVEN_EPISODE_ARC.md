## Voice rules for the next implementation

- Each scene contains one ordinary object, one concrete task and one visible obstruction. Chief’s elevated wording is the excess; the player is not the punchline.
- Give at least two choices a real situational benefit. The full form can help someone organize a difficult request even when it delays the next brief. Fast recovery can be defensible while diagnosis remains open.
- One sharp line follows the consequence. Keep useful action and return invitation in plain language. Avoid a second joke that obscures what changed.
- Let respect, uncertainty and revision appear as often as a roast. A useful failure is not a humiliation. Do not mock someone for giving feedback or declining to share.
- Preserve local-save boundaries in both copy and implementation. These stories are related; they do not all exchange state. Coffee currently resets on reload. Quiet Hour and cupboard handover have separate local saves.
- No automatic sharing, generic “join our movement” ending or promised reply. One optional next action is enough. Do not use player engagement as a token-launch claim.
